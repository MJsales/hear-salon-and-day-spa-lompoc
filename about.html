About

A Lompoc Fixture, One Cut at a Time

Clean, comfortable, and family-run — on East Ocean Avenue since long before anyone needed a website to find us.

Shear Salon & Day Spa sits on East Ocean Avenue offering cuts, color, ombré, perms, and spa services in a space regulars describe as relaxing, clean, and welcoming — for kids getting their first haircut and for the husband who normally avoids salons altogether.

Guests can also book facials, waxing, brow shaping, and NovaLash-certified lash extensions on-site through resident esthetics partner HoneyB Esthetics.
